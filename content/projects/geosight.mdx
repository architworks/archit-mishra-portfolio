## What it does

GeoSight combines a vector world map with LLM reasoning to make a live map of global politics. It knows which alliances were active in 1960 versus 2026, and it pulls in current developments as they happen.

- **Interactive map:** zoom, pan, and click through countries, with layers for military and economic alliances and active conflicts.
- **Timeline:** switch between eras from 1914 through the Cold War to 2026.
- **Live intel ticker:** current headlines fetched through Gemini Search Grounding.
- **AI analyst:** a chat sidebar that stays aware of the map's current state.
- **Alliance comparison:** a visual way to compare overlapping memberships.

## Under the hood

- **Stack:** React 19, TypeScript, and Vite, with D3.js for the map.
- **Context injection:** changing the year updates the analyst's context, so it talks about the Cold War when the map shows the Cold War.
- **On-demand summaries:** country, alliance, and conflict panels are generated when you open them.
