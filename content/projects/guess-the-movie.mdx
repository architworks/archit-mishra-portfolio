## How it plays

Add your players, set the kind of movies you're in the mood for, and choose the rounds, hints, and timer. Each card is an image generated for a real film.

- Everyone shares one screen, guesses aloud, and types an answer.
- Wrong guesses keep the answer hidden, and hints open one at a time.
- A correct guess reveals the film and awards a point to the player you pick.

## Under the hood

- **Gemini for the game:** the API creates each puzzle and judges answers, and the AI picks films freely while rejecting duplicates within a game.
- **No database:** the browser keeps the game, images, and scores in IndexedDB, so a closed tab can pick up where it left off.
- **Protected answers:** the answer and hints travel in an authenticated, encrypted token, and the API key stays on the server.
- **Always a card ready:** new cards generate while players are still guessing the current one.
