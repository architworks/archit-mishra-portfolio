## What it does

Kitch keeps one household's kitchen in one place. Meal plans, pantry stock, recipes, and grocery carts are shared across the household, while nutrition logs stay personal to each member.

- Plans and edits meals for exact dates and ranges.
- Generates recipes and builds the shopping list from them, leaving out what's already in the pantry.
- Reads and updates pantry stock from text or a photo.
- Logs and corrects nutrition entries from text or meal photos.
- Remembers household context in natural language across sessions.

## How it's built

- **Hierarchical agents:** a coordinator agent built on Google ADK and Gemini delegates to least-privilege specialist agents, including a sandboxed multimodal vision agent.
- **Long-term memory:** household context persists through Vertex AI Memory Bank.
- **Human approval:** agents can prepare a live delivery cart, but payment and checkout stay outside their toolset, behind explicit approval in the interface.
- **Stack:** a Next.js frontend, a FastAPI backend, and Supabase for hosted state with backend-only row-level security.

## Security

I threat-modelled the agent system, mapped 104 attack surfaces, and closed all High and Medium findings. That work covered prompt-injection containment, tenant isolation, encrypted tokens, data retention, and per-run caps on runaway AI loops.
