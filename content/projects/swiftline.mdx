## What it does

Rapid serial visual presentation keeps your eyes in one place and brings the words to you, so they don't wander. Swiftline turns that idea into a reader you can come back to.

- **Adaptive speed:** go from 200 to 800+ words a minute with fine-tuned controls.
- **Any format:** upload PDFs, EPUBs, DOCX, Markdown, or plain text.
- **Smart bookmarks:** save your place and annotate any moment in the text.
- **Persistent progress:** pick up where you stopped.

## Under the hood

- **Stack:** Next.js with Supabase for accounts and saved progress.
- **Document parsing:** PDF, EPUB, and Word files are read with `pdfjs`, `epubjs`, and `mammoth`.
